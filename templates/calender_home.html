Land Surface Temperature Dashboard

This dashboard helps you explore how land‑surface temperature (LST) patterns vary across Indian cities — and why they matter for public‑health planning.

What This Dashboard Offers

  • Time‑series temperature trends
  • Multi‑year daytime averages
  • Urban vs rural surface temps
  • Greenness (NDVI) correlation
  • Health & population overlays

Data & Methodology

  • LANDSAT 8, MODIS LST
  • Google Earth Engine processing
  • High‑res thermal imagery
  • NDVI, LST, UTFVI indices
  • Ward‑level geostatistics

Why It Matters

  • Urban‑heat‑island monitoring
  • Identify vulnerable wards
  • Support heat‑action plans
  • Track greening initiatives
